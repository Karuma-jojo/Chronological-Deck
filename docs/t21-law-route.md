# T21 — Law, Jurisprudence & Legal Reasoning

T21 is an additive terminal route for Chrono-Deck v1.1. It leaves T01–T20 unchanged and extends the World Registry from 630 to 710 nodes.

## Contract

- **Terminal ID:** `T21`
- **Umbrella:** Law, Jurisprudence & Public Institutions
- **Route size:** 91 required nodes
- **New law nodes:** 80 (`ARC631`–`ARC710`)
- **Reused Chrono-Deck nodes:** 11 reasoning/evidence foundations
- **Scientific core:** the frozen 39-node scientific core remains the shared core for T01–T20. T21 is deliberately separate and reuses only relevant reasoning/evidence nodes instead of forcing the full STEM core.

## T21 finishing gate

To clear T21, the learner must be able to analyze an unseen fact pattern; distinguish material from immaterial facts; find and rank authority; construct the strongest arguments for opposing sides; identify hidden premises and fallacies; interpret statutes, constitutions and precedents using competing methods; reason about causation, evidence, burdens and standards of proof; choose and justify remedies and procedural routes; produce a research-backed brief; conduct an oral moot under hostile questioning; and write a reasoned judgment that squarely addresses counterarguments, institutional consequences and professional ethics.

The final synthesis is `ARC710 — Capstone: Integrated Legal Problem, Brief & Judgment`.

## Decision

Rebuild M04 as a **28-session** candidate. Preserve the stable identities `S01–S24`, insert four separately assessable foundations as `S25–S28`, and change only learner-facing `order` positions. Do not treat the historical v2.1 confirmation as certification of this expanded contract.

The four additions have different central learner decisions:

- **S25 · Finite probability bounds & incomplete joint information** — decide what is exact, what is bounded, and what would require an extra dependence assumption.
- **S26 · Longer finite paths & full-history conditioning** — keep the complete positive-mass history under each conditional bar and distinguish independence from identical trial probabilities.
- **S27 · Observation & reporting mechanisms** — make the selector/reporter part of the finite experiment and condition on the actual observation event.
- **S28 · Finite stopped experiments & terminal prefixes** — enumerate the real bounded terminal experiment, not equal-looking terminal labels or duplicated continuations.

## Ownership delta

### S25 owns

1. event monotonicity from a disjoint difference;
2. finite union bound without independence;
3. sharp two-event overlap/union ranges from marginals;
4. legal endpoint region-mass constructions;
5. rejection of exact joint answers not identified by the supplied marginals.

It explicitly does **not** own Markov/Chebyshev/Hoeffding or formal joint-distribution machinery.

### S26 owns

1. repeated multiplication for three-or-more-stage finite paths;
2. full-history conditioning labels;
3. outgoing-branch and terminal normalization audits;
4. independence versus identical-distribution discrimination;
5. summation of unequal path masses.

It does **not** import the Markov property, likelihood theory, policies or transition matrices.

### S27 owns

1. public specification of the observation protocol;
2. enlargement to `(underlying record, reporter choice)` atoms;
3. exact definition of the observation event;
4. direct finite conditioning on that event;
5. comparison of different mechanisms that can produce similar surface messages.

Bayes as a reusable inversion theorem, causal identification and missing-data correction remain downstream.

### S28 owns

1. bounded stopping rules;
2. exhaustive/disjoint terminal prefixes;
3. prefix path masses;
4. aggregation without recounting continuations;
5. probability-preserving padded fixed-horizon checks.

Martingales, optional stopping, unbounded waiting and dynamic programming remain downstream.

# M03 v1.2 evidence retrofit

Status: **repair candidate — independent follow-up required**

Branch: `codex/m03-spire-bounded-repair`

This is a bounded retrofit of the existing 30-session M03 architecture. It does not expand M03's mathematical scope.

## Frozen architecture

- 30 sessions.
- 60 stable task IDs.
- 150 ownership claims.
- M03 stops at deterministic finite reasoning/counting; M04 owns probability.
- General finite binomial theorem remains owned by S26 because M10 consumes it.

## Why the retrofit was required

The hostile book-grounded audit found that the older M03 evidence wrapper could remain green while overstating learner evidence:

- 24 Transfer rubrics were generic boilerplate rather than task-specific observers;
- several ownership claims were not literally requested by their public task;
- the learner-pack method-recognition gates pre-exposed the exact assessed statements;
- five Transfer slots were retrieval/near-clones rather than meaningful changed-surface work;
- S26 printed the theorem formula while awarding credit for 'stating' it;
- S27's raw stars-and-bars string was Markdown-sensitive;
- semantic-separation receipts described older instruction rather than the current lesson;
- the old audit lacked canonical evidence-distance, decision and wrong-solver ledgers.

## Assessment repairs

- S07-M v2: the learner must describe the counterexample search family before presenting the counterexample.

### Weak Transfer replacements

Stable task IDs are retained with obligationVersion 2:

- S17-T: explicit listed sets → property-defined sets inside a finite universe.

- S24-T: ordered selections are reconstructed as injective finite maps.

S30-T is obligationVersion 3: it now honestly **uses** the three-set inclusion-exclusion rule owned by S28 rather than claiming that rule is unintroduced.

## Evaluator repair

All 24 former boilerplate Transfer rubrics are replaced by task-specific mathematical criteria. No surviving Transfer evaluator contains the generic rows:

- "Correct changed-surface result."
- "Uses the intended underlying capability."
- "Shows auditable reasoning."
- "Respects all stated assumptions/domains."
- "States the requested conclusion precisely."

Any evaluator change that alters evidence meaning is versioned. Prior attempts remain attached to their historical assessment fingerprints and do not silently recertify a new obligation.

## Evidence semantics

Canonical metadata now includes:

- `evidenceDistance` for all 60 fixed tasks using only retrieval / proof reconstruction / fresh Main evidence / changed-surface Transfer;
- `decisionAudit` for tasks that genuinely claim a changed surface or organizing decision;
- `wrongSolverAudit` for all 30 sessions;
- a rebuilt `semanticSeparationAudit` tied to the current instruction version;
- a coverage status that explicitly says independent semantic reconfirmation is still pending.

Different numbers are not accepted as proof of semantic separation.

## Strategy-choice practice

The contaminated pre-assessment method gates are removed from the learner pack.

They are replaced by two fresh, unscored Spire strategy labs:

1. after S14, interleaving proof methods on never-assessed statements;
2. after S30, interleaving finite-counting tools on never-assessed situations.

The labs are diagnostic/practice only and cannot auto-grant canonical ownership credit.

This follows the source-stack distinction between learning a named proof architecture and learning to choose among plausible strategies.

## Source / pedagogy ledger

- Daniel Velleman, *How to Prove It*: logical form and proof-strategy selection; multiple strategies may need to be tried before a useful one is found.
- Richard Hammack, *Book of Proof*: beginner proof progression through direct proof, contrapositive, contradiction, induction, sets, relations and functions.
- Lehman, Leighton & Meyer, *Mathematics for Computer Science*: clear proof game plans, linear argument flow and discrete-mathematics comparators.
- Paul Zeitz, *The Art and Craft of Problem Solving*: exercises versus genuine problems, orientation, strategy/tactics/tools and contest-style reconstruction.
- MAA *Instructional Practices Guide*: general undergraduate-mathematics design comparator; not claimed as direct outcome evidence for M03.
- IES/WWC *Organizing Instruction and Study to Improve Student Learning*: general worked-example/practice/interleaving comparator; not claimed as direct validation of this curriculum.

# M02 boundary & authoring contract — T22E-FND02

Status: **semantic boundary accepted; authored content candidate; not learner-facing until all gates pass**

Prerequisite module: **M01 · T22E-FND01 only**.

## Ownership

M02 owns functions as objects, domains/ranges, coordinate graphs and lines, piecewise rules, transformations, composition, elementary inverses, polynomial/rational/root families, exponentials/logarithms, log returns, finite arithmetic/geometric sequences, sigma notation, elementary recurrences **without convergence claims**, and only the radians/unit-circle/trigonometry needed downstream.

M02 explicitly does **not** own formal logic/proofs/sets/combinatorics (M03), limits/convergence/infinite series (M09), calculus, probability or general complex-number theory.

## Added session contracts

### S25 · finite bounds / incomplete joint information

Owns event monotonicity, the finite union bound, sharp two-event overlap/union feasibility ranges, endpoint constructions and assumption discipline.

Pinned diagnostics:

- `P(A)=.60, P(B)=.50` ⇒ `.10≤P(A∩B)≤.50`
- corresponding union range `.60≤P(A∪B)≤1`
- three failure marginals `.15,.20,.30` ⇒ guaranteed union range `.30≤P(any)≤.65`, with nested/disjoint constructions attaining endpoints.

### S26 · full-history paths

Owns finite chain products with the full positive-mass history written under each conditional bar, leaf normalization and independence-versus-identical-distribution discrimination.

Pinned diagnostics:

- eight-leaf Main normalizes exactly and gives `P(success)=.48`
- independent heterogeneous success probabilities `.20,.50,.80` give exactly-one probability `.42`
- the average-`p` constant-trial shortcut gives `.375` and is explicitly rejected.

### S27 · observation/reporting mechanisms

Owns public specification of the reporter/selector, enlarged finite atom spaces, exact observation events and direct finite conditioning.

Pinned diagnostics:

- whole-record filter on two fair fields: `P(HH | at least one H)=1/3`
- uniformly selected reported field: `P(HH | selected field reports H)=1/2`
- unequal-selector Transfer: `P(HH | reported H)=4/13`, versus direct record filtering `1/6`.

### S28 · finite stopped experiments

Owns bounded stopping rules, exhaustive/disjoint terminal prefixes, unequal prefix masses and padded fixed-horizon cross-checks.

Pinned diagnostics:

- fair first-H-or-cap-3 terminals: `1/2,1/4,1/8,1/8`, success by cap `7/8`
- first-to-two contest with `P(A wins a round)=.60`: contest win probability `.648`.

## Retained-session cleanup

The old module was not indiscriminately rewritten. The bounded upgrades are:

- **S03** now deliberately reactivates M03 fibres/preimages and separates counting from a probability law.
- **S07** keeps null-denominator discipline and forward-links to S27's information-mechanism distinction.
- **S15/S16** explicitly separate independence from identical per-position probabilities; S26 supplies the hard discriminator.
- **S19 Main** is versioned to obligation v2 and now scores payoff units plus the finite weighted-mean range check.
- **S22** explicitly grounds the positional inclusion proof in M03-S34/S35 uniform-fibre/double-counting reasoning.
- **S24 Transfer** is versioned to obligation v4 as the expanded-route cumulative exit challenge: it propagates incomplete joint information through expectation and rejects both independence-by-default and an unsupported policy-optimality conclusion.

Historical v2.1 attempts are therefore not silently treated as evidence for materially changed v2.2 assessment obligations.

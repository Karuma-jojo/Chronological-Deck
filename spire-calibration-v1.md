# Spire Compass · Calibration v1.0 (frozen 2026-10-27)

## Purpose
Distinguish **mission completion** from **evidence of broad reusable mathematical competence**. A reviewed problem is not a completed skill seal. This contract applies to the 42 fixed Spire seals in seven weighted gates; it is not an SMMC percentile or mastery certification.

## Score calculation
For each gate G with weight W and six seals, score(G) = W * (sum of six seal tiers)/6.
Levels: unassessed 0, introduced 0.10, assisted 0.30, independent 0.70, retained_transfer 1.00. The displayed Spire Ascent is sum of the seven gate scores, out of 100. G3 weighs 16: two seals at introduced contribute 16*(0.1+0.1)/6 = 0.5333, displayed 0.5/100 or 0.5/16 within G3. Two assessed seals are not two completed seals.

## Promotion criteria — freeze now
- **Unassessed · 0:** No verified applicable evidence. Does not mean inability.
- **Introduced · 0.10:** At least one correctly understood and completed introductory/tutorial task. Record the exact assistance, proof, and source. This can be a simple task and is not independent-transfer credit.
- **Assisted · 0.30:** At least three successfully solved varied tasks for the same seal, at least one **fresh medium challenge** (not merely a disguised copy of a recently taught exercise); learner builds sound general proofs. Coaching/hints permitted but explicitly recorded. Require substantial coverage of the stated seal, not a single narrow trick repeated.
- **Independent · 0.70:** At least three varied unseen tasks of genuine medium-or-greater difficulty, in at least two separate sessions, all solved with self-selected strategy and complete argument without strategic hints or proof-repair guidance. Simple rule clarification is allowed. Require multiple manifestations of the competency (e.g., both invariants and monovariants where relevant).
- **Retained transfer · 1.00:** At least two *additional*, varied unseen problems at least seven days after relevant practice, without hints, including one timed task at appropriate competition difficulty. Evidence covers the whole seal criterion.
All thresholds are minimum evidence requirements; meeting numerical counts alone never overrides mathematical errors or narrow task coverage.

## Non-negotiable accounting
1. A **mission** records that a problem was solved and how; it does not add a fixed amount of ascent XP.
2. Store problem difficulty, source (tutorial/original/textbook/SMMC PYQ), assistance, dates, solution, errors and repairs, and transfer gap.
3. Count correlated practice carefully. The same recently demonstrated parity invariant applied to an elementary lamp puzzle is not two independent medium transfer results merely because it touches parity and invariants.
4. A strategic insight independently identified during an easy tutorial is recognized in the journal but does not justify the entire broad competency being graded Independent.
5. Never silently move thresholds, add new seals, reweight gates, or invent new evidence. Only a user-approved calibration/version change may alter the contract; historical evidence remains intact.
6. Distinguish site-local progress from authentic cloud synchronization. Review notes and provenance must be auditable. No fabricated official SMMC performances.

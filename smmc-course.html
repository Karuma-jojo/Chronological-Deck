Algebra into problem solving

Begin after M01 and M02 through S09. Twelve original exercises move from focused practice to mixed challenges. No calculus, number theory or past-paper exposure required.

Practise choosing and combining familiar ideas. These are foundation exercises, not a claim to match SMMC difficulty.

Browse the six chapter steps
How to use this chapter

Learn an idea, try the guided check, then save your reasoning on the practice problems. When the support fades, choose your own approach. If a definition or calculation is missing, revisit the lesson; if you know the tools but cannot see a route, try small cases and write what you notice before requesting a hint.

The mixed sets ask you to recombine familiar mathematics without a method label. Getting stuck is expected. The target is increasingly independent reasoning, not a perfect streak or finishing every question quickly.

These steps describe the intended progression, not a guarantee that every next problem will feel harder or easier to you.

After this chapter

Finish M02, then build proof and counting with M03. The later contest chapters will connect that foundation to suitable past papers. Completing twelve algebra exercises alone does not establish PYQ readiness.
